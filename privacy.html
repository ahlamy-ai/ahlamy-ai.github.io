Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Ahlamy handles personal information when you visit our website, create an account, use a workspace or contact us.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use Ahlamy, we may collect your name and email address, account authentication details, company or workspace name, country, industry, preferred language, messages you send to us, and information needed to operate and support your account. We may also receive basic technical and usage information needed to secure and maintain the website and application.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and manage accounts and workspaces, provide requested services, respond to enquiries, maintain security, troubleshoot issues, process subscription status, and meet legal obligations.

3. Service providers

We use service providers to operate Ahlamy. Supabase provides database, authentication and application infrastructure. Paddle may process checkout, payments, subscription billing and related customer information as Merchant of Record. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy documentation.

4. Payments

When checkout is provided by Paddle, payment card details are entered into Paddle's checkout and processed by Paddle. Ahlamy receives limited transaction and subscription information needed to associate a purchase with your workspace and provide the relevant service. We do not ask you to send payment card details by email.

5. Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. We share information with service providers where needed to operate Ahlamy, and where required by law or to protect rights and security. We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations.

6. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements and records we must retain. Contact contact.ahlamy@gmail.com. You can also stop using the service and request account closure through that address.

7. Security and international processing

We use service providers and reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information. Information may be processed in countries where our providers operate, subject to applicable safeguards. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Children

Ahlamy is intended for business users and is not directed to children.

9. Updates and contact

We may update this policy by publishing a new version here. For privacy questions, contact contact.ahlamy@gmail.com.
