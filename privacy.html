AHLAMY — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

1. Introduction

AHLAMY operates AHLAMY Universal AI, a software platform that provides AI-powered customer communication tools to businesses, including businesses operating in France and the European Union.

We aim to protect personal data in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Information We Collect

  • Names and email addresses.
  • Account and business information.
  • Customer conversations and messages.
  • Products, services and business information submitted to the platform.
  • Technical information needed for security and platform operation.

3. How We Use Personal Data

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Provide AI-powered customer communication services.
  • Manage subscriptions and payments.
  • Provide technical support.
  • Maintain platform security.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

4. Legal Bases for Processing

Depending on the purpose, processing may rely on contractual necessity, legal obligations, legitimate interests or consent where required.

5. Data Sharing

Personal data may be shared with authorized service providers where necessary to operate the platform, including hosting, authentication, AI and payment providers. We do not sell personal data.

6. International Data Transfers

Where personal data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, appropriate legal safeguards will be implemented as required by applicable law.

7. Data Retention

Personal data will be retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal obligations.

8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, individuals may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of their personal data, and request data portability. They may also lodge a complaint with the competent authority, including the CNIL in France.

9. Security

We aim to implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration or misuse.

10. Contact

For privacy-related requests, contact contact.ahlamy@gmail.com.
