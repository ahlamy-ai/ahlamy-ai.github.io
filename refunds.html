Refund & Cancellation Policy

This policy applies to subscriptions and paid services offered by Ahlamy. The specific plan, price, billing frequency, renewal date and any setup fee are shown at checkout or agreed in writing before you place an order.

1. Subscription cancellation

To ask to cancel a subscription or prevent a future renewal, contact contact.ahlamy@gmail.com using the email address associated with your account. Include your workspace name and, if available, the subscription or transaction reference. We will confirm the next steps and any applicable effective date. Cancellation does not automatically create a refund for charges already made.

2. Refund requests

If you believe you are entitled to a refund, contact contact.ahlamy@gmail.com and include your order reference, the date of purchase and a short explanation. We review requests in accordance with applicable consumer law, the terms presented at checkout and the payment provider's procedures. Nothing in this policy limits any mandatory statutory refund or cancellation rights.

3. Paddle payments

Where Paddle processes your payment, Paddle acts as Merchant of Record and may administer the payment and refund process. You may be directed to Paddle's buyer support for assistance with a payment or refund. Please retain your checkout confirmation or receipt so the transaction can be identified.

4. Service issues

If you cannot access a service you purchased, contact us at contact.ahlamy@gmail.com with a description of the issue. We will review the issue and work with you on an appropriate resolution.

5. Contact

For cancellation or refund questions, email contact.ahlamy@gmail.com.
