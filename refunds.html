AHLAMY — Refund Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

1. General Policy

AHLAMY aims to provide clear subscription and billing terms. Refund requests are handled in accordance with applicable purchase terms and mandatory consumer protection laws.

2. Free Trial

Eligible customers may receive a 7-day free trial with limited usage. A paid subscription will not begin automatically without the customer's explicit approval.

3. Subscription Payments

Subscription prices and billing periods will be displayed before checkout. Customers should review their selected plan and payment details before confirming a purchase.

4. Cancellation

Customers may request subscription cancellation. Unless applicable law or the purchase terms provide otherwise, cancellation prevents future renewals but does not automatically refund previous payments.

5. Refund Requests

Refund requests may be submitted to our support email. Please include the account email address, transaction reference and a brief explanation.

6. European Consumer Rights

Where a customer qualifies as a consumer under French or European Union law, mandatory statutory rights apply. This may include a 14-day right of withdrawal for eligible distance contracts, subject to applicable legal conditions and exceptions.

Where a customer requests immediate commencement of a service or digital content during a withdrawal period, any legally required express consent and acknowledgment will be obtained.

Nothing in this policy removes mandatory statutory rights.

7. Billing Errors

Customers who believe they have been charged incorrectly should contact our support team with their transaction details.

8. Contact

For refund and billing requests, contact contact.ahlamy@gmail.com.
