# Ahlamy Universal AI

A multilingual, multi-tenant AI platform for businesses worldwide.

## Core Principles
- One central AI platform for multiple companies.
- Strict data isolation between companies.
- Arabic, French, and English support.
- Secure authentication and role-based permissions.
- Independent knowledge bases and dashboards for each company.
- Multi-branch support.
- Integrations for products, services, bookings, payments, and locations.
- Human approval for sensitive operations.

## Platform Modules
- Ahlamy AI
- Ahlamy Digital
- Ahlamy Commerce
- Ahlamy Business
- Ahlamy Academy
- Ahlamy Global

## Development Priorities
1. Secure project architecture.
2. Database and tenant isolation.
3. Authentication and permissions.
4. AI engine and company knowledge bases.
5. Business dashboards.
6. Integrations and automation.
7. Testing, monitoring, and security.
